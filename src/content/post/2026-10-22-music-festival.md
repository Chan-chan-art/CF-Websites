Major Global Festivals 
a community gathering or organized event featuring live musical performances

- **Tomorrowland:** A massive electronic dance music (EDM) festival held in Belgium.
- **Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival:** A multi-genre destination festival held annually in California, USA.
- **Glastonbury Festival:** A historic contemporary performing arts and music festival in the United Kingdom.
- **Rock in Rio:** One of the largest music and rock festivals hosted across Brazil and Portugal.
