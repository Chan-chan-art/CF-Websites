### Show Overview
- **Concept:** 60 South Korean rappers live and compete in an isolated society, using rap battles as their primary weapon for survival, strategy, blocks (teams), and psychological warfare.
- **Prize:** A grand prize of 300 million won (approx. $300,000 USD) for the winning block.
- **Release Date:** Aired from October 1 to December 10, 2024, across 12 episodes on TVING.
